# T87: What it is, from the bands

- **Owner:** (open, suggested Joerg)  ·  **Status:** open
- **Depends on / feeds:** T85 (field spectra), T86, T63 (feature list in `web/spectrum.js`)

## Goal
Per source, measure the features SPHEREx can see at R ≈ 35–130 and turn them into a class or hint:
continuum temperature, IR excess (disk / dust), ice absorption (H2O 3.0, CO2 4.27, CO 4.67 µm), CH4 3.3 µm
(T/Y dwarfs), H2O 1.4 / 1.9 µm (M/L dwarfs), CO 2.3 µm (giants), PAH 3.3 µm and Pa-α 1.875 / Br-α 4.05 µm
(star formation), the redshifted 1.6 µm bump of galaxies (rough redshift). Narrow features need the full
spectra (T66/T85). Later, maps of a feature (e.g. ice depth over a cloud) are a note to the tile owner (Jonathan).
