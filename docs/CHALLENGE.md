## Our understanding (unofficial)

Since 2025, SPHEREx has mapped the whole sky about every 6 months in 102 near-infrared bands
(0.75–5 µm). Things that move between epochs (asteroids, comets, brown dwarfs, maybe a Planet X)
show up as position shifts. Goal: a public tool that lets anyone see how the sky changes over time
in SPHEREx data and spot movers.

Likely data sources:

- IRSA: SPHEREx quick releases (QR2/QR3) via SIA/TAP, cutouts
- JPL Horizons: SPHEREx's own position (ID `-163182`)
- JPL Small-Body Identification: known small bodies in a field at a given time
