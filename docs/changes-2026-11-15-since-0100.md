# Features and fixes since 01:00 (2026-11-15)

About 40 changes from 01:00 to 07:00: roughly 30 from Mati's session and 10 from Joerg's. Jonathan's session pushed
nothing in that window. Task numbers refer to the Board in `TEAM.md` and the files in `tasks/`.

## New features

### Detection

- **Mover detection (T06).** Finds sources that are in the images at one moment, seen by both detectors that image
  at once, and gone at other times. Since T106 it runs on every field, including fields built earlier. On the
  cached fields it found 3 candidates; nobody has looked at them by eye yet.
- **Detector artifacts (T67).** Afterglows of bright stars and hot pixels are marked as artifacts instead of
  candidates.
- **Stars that change brightness (T61).** Compares each catalogued star between exposures at the same wavelength.
  Shown as a "Changes brightness" list and as dashed "V" rings on the map. On two Ophiuchus fields, 24 of the
  34 flagged stars are known variables; the other 10 are new candidates.

### Chemistry maps

- **Combined picture (T96).** Several features shown as one colour picture.
- **Presets (T107).** "Ionised gas", "Ices in cold clouds" and "Lit cloud edges", each tried on Orion, ρ Oph and
  NGC 7023, with how to read the colours and what can fool them.
- **No limit of 3 features (T108).** From 4 features on, each gets its own colour from blue to red, with a warning
  that mixed colours become ambiguous.
- **Redshift for galaxies (T109).** A "Redshift z" box, filled in from SIMBAD when a galaxy sits at the centre.
  On NGC 6240, without it the Br-α map even showed the wrong sign. Joerg's session restricted the lookup to real
  galaxies (T112).

### Other

- **Quality flags (T60, Joerg's session).** The SPHEREx quality flags at a clicked spot, and a "Quality flags"
  mask on the map.
- **Blink switch (T100).** Stops the map's automatic blinking; a click on a date shows that one.
